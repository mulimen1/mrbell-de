Questions

When do I get paid?

A purchase is confirmed 14 days after it was made, because until then parents can use the money-back guarantee. Once a month we pay out all confirmed commissions, with no minimum, by PayPal or bank transfer. We ask for your payout details by email before the first payout.

What happens if a parent gets a refund?

Then there's no commission for that purchase. You'll see it as "refunded" in your numbers.

How do I see what I've earned?

Further down this page under "Your numbers", with your code and your personal key. Every purchase is listed individually with date and time, without any buyer data.

Do I need to label my posts as ads?

Yes. Because you earn a commission, FTC rules require you to clearly disclose it, for example with #ad or the paid partnership label on Instagram and TikTok.

What about taxes?

Commissions are income, so taxes are on your side. You get a statement for every payout for your records.

How long is my code valid?

As long as the partner program runs. If the terms change, we'll tell you by email at least 30 days in advance.
