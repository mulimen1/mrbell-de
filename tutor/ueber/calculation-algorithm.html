Our calculation algorithm

Why this matters to us

In the exam, the result counts. One wrong number in your working costs marks, and if you learn from a wrong example, you learn the mistake too. AI models explain well, but they sometimes miscalculate. That’s why we don’t rely on AI for numbers.

An algorithm that doesn’t guess

We built our own calculation algorithm. It works without AI, calculates exactly and without rounding errors, and understands how workings are written in business and economics: formulas, intermediate steps, percentages, journal entries, tables with totals. It reads both German and English number formats.

Everything on the site is checked in advance

  • Calculators and the BWL trainer are hard-coded and were cross-checked by the algorithm with thousands of random tasks.
  • Excel templates were tested with thousands of inputs, against several independent calculation engines and with the real .xlsx file.
  • Study pages, boards, glossary and quiz questions: every calculation in them is recalculated, and every journal entry has to balance.
  • Only what passes the check with zero open findings goes live.

What happens to Max’s answers

When Max explains or calculates something for you, his answer automatically runs through the algorithm before you see it. That applies to the chat, the board, quizzes, flashcards, study sheets, lessons and podcasts.

  • Checked: every calculation Max shows, including formulas, workings in the text, journal entries, totals and quiz solutions.
  • Corrected: a mistake, as soon as it’s clear what is wrong. Later steps that depend on it are corrected too.
  • Reworked if that isn’t clear. Max recalculates exactly that spot and the algorithm checks again.
  • Flagged: anything that still doesn’t add up gets a warning sign saying our calculation algorithm couldn’t verify it. You’ll never see an unchecked number presented as verified.

Below an answer you can see how many calculations were checked. This usually takes just milliseconds.

In the Excel helper

The templates are fully tested in advance, see above. When Max builds your own file in the generator, your browser recalculates every formula and a second, independent pass checks the results. Where a ratio isn’t defined, such as a division by zero, you see “–” instead of a made-up number.

What the algorithm doesn’t check

The algorithm checks the maths, not the content. It can’t know whether a definition matches your lecture notes or whether a formula is the one your professor uses. How we review content is explained in our editorial principles (German). If in doubt, your course’s version applies.

No guarantee, liability

We check carefully and put a lot of effort into it. Even so, mistakes can never be ruled out entirely, not even by an algorithm. We therefore give no guarantee that calculations or content are correct, complete or up to date. For exams, assignments and anything else where a result matters, you remain responsible yourself. Check important results against your lecture notes. Our liability is governed by section 10 of our terms (German).
